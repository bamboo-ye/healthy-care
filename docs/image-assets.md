# 健康主题插画素材

制作方式为内置图像生成工具。以下两份素材用于用户端，保持参考项目的柔和手绘插画语言，但采用健康主题绿色配色，不展示其他业务模块。

## 角色logo提示词

Use case: stylized-concept / logo-brand.
Asset type: a square mascot logo illustration for the standalone Chinese everyday health assistant named 伴AI·健康伙伴. Do not put any text in the image.
Input image: the attached original companion mascot is a style and character-family reference only.
Primary request: draw a new green health companion in exactly the same soft, rounded, polished pastel illustration family: a cute cloud-haired little AI buddy, large kind dark eyes, tiny smiling mouth, subtle blush, padded headphones and a tiny antenna. Preserve the cloud hair, expressive face proportions, friendly headset silhouette, hand-painted highlights and gently outlined shapes. Change blue/pink dominant accents into sage, mint and soft jade green. A small leaf antenna and a pale green heart on the outfit connect to everyday health and care. No doctor costume, syringe, pill, hospital sign or medical authority symbol.
Composition: one centered upper-body mascot, comfortably large and readable as a sidebar logo and a small welcome illustration, head and earphones fully inside the canvas, no extra surrounding objects, minimal subtle shadow. Transparent background, clean edges. No text, lettering, watermark or frame.

## 用户端背景提示词

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a wide 16:9 desktop application background illustration, not a UI screenshot.
Input image: the attached original pastel pink companion room is a reference for composition, material, softness and hand-painted visual style.
Primary request: redraw this cozy airy room as a serene pale green everyday health companion environment. Match the same rounded soft hand-painted storybook aesthetic, translucent watercolor-like shading, creamy highlights, delicate outlines and low contrast. Preserve a window and soft curtains on the left, a rounded comfortable sofa in the bottom left, a soft rug along the bottom, and a small side table with a cup and a leafy potted plant toward the bottom right. Recolor the scene from pink to mint, sage and creamy pistachio green, with a few very pale warm cream details. Small leaf-like accents may replace the floating hearts. No people, characters, medical apparatus, medicine bottles or hospital setting.
Composition for the existing app shell: keep the central and upper central 65 percent extremely quiet, pale and almost empty so translucent white content cards remain readable. Objects stay at the outer left and lower corners; no high-contrast details behind the central dashboard. Wide landscape composition. No text, UI panels, logo, border or watermark.
